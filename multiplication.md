How to Multiply Two Integers:

Line up the two numbers vertically, placing the larger number on top.
Multiply the bottom number's ones digit by every digit in the top number, working right to left.
Write down the result. If a multiplication results in a two-digit number, carry the tens digit over.
If multiplying by a tens digit, add a placeholder zero on the next line before multiplying.
Add the resulting rows together to get the final answer.
Example: 12 x 13 12 x 10

+120 (12 x 10, using a placeholder zero)

120 (The final product)
